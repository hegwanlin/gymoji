Privacy Policy

繁體中文 English

Last updated: October 2026

Gymoji ("the App") is developed by Kuan-Lin Ho. Your privacy matters. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Storage

All workout records in the App (including exercises, weights, reps, workout times, nutrition status, and so on) are stored locally on your own device. They're never uploaded to any of the developer's servers, and the developer can't access, view, or obtain any of your data.

Data Sharing

The App doesn't share any of your data with third parties and doesn't use any advertising or analytics services.

Data Backup, Export, and Import

The App offers four ways to handle your data, all of which you choose to use within the App:

  • Offline full backup and restore: Export a full backup file on your phone (it includes your workouts, routines, custom exercises and their photos, and settings), and restore from it.
  • iCloud full backup and restore: Save a full backup to your own iCloud account and restore from it. This data is stored and handled by Apple's iCloud service under Apple's privacy policy, and the developer can't access it.
  • CSV export and import: Export or import your workouts as CSV files, which open in any spreadsheet app.
  • Import from Strong: Choose a CSV file you exported from Strong, and the App reads and imports it on your device.

Backup files and CSV files are handled solely through the iOS share sheet and file picker and are stored wherever you choose. None of these options sends your data through the developer's servers.

Before restoring from a backup file, the App automatically saves a copy of your current data on your device. You can view or delete it in the Files app under On My iPhone › Gymoji.
