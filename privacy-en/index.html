Privacy Policy

繁體中文 English

Last updated: October 2026

Gymoji ("the App") is developed by Kuan-Lin Ho. Your privacy matters. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Storage

All workout records in the App (including exercises, weights, reps, workout times, nutrition status, and so on) are stored only locally on your own device. They're never uploaded to any server, and the developer can't access, view, or obtain any of your data.

Data Sharing

The App doesn't share any of your data with third parties and doesn't use any advertising or analytics services.

Data Export, Import, and Backup

The App lets you export and import your workouts as CSV files, and export and restore full backup files (which include your workouts, routines, custom exercises and their photos, and settings). These happen only when you trigger them manually. Your data is handled solely through the iOS share sheet and file picker, is stored wherever you choose, and never passes through the developer's servers.

Before restoring from a backup file, the App automatically saves a copy of your current data on your device. You can view or delete it in the Files app under On My iPhone › Gymoji.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email hegwanlin@gmail.com
